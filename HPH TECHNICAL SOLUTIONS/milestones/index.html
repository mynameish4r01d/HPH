Milestones

A decade-plus of growth, partnerships, and new capabilities.

2013

In November, HPH Technical Solutions Inc. was established.

HPH TSI became a value provider for preventive maintenance of AE and CE properties.

2014

HPH TSI incorporated the latest NDT equipment to aid in predictive maintenance, such as thermal imaging and PQA, and expanded services to a leading BPO company.

2015

Established a division for fit-out and renovation works, catering to BPO companies, residentials, spas, and foreign-owned properties.

Partnered with ABB to distribute and support their CWA and measurement products.

2017

Partnered with L&W and Teledyne to distribute and support their products.

2021

Relocated to our new office in 17 Vatican Building, enabling in-house training for our customers.

2025

Seeing the need for better solutions in renewable energy, HPH Renewable was formed, partnering with Projoy and TSUN.
