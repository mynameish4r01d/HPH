Company Profile

Managed and supervised by engineers and facilities professionals, not just contractors.

HPH Technical Solutions Incorporated was established in 2013 and is managed and supervised by engineers and facilities professionals, consistently trained in seminars and certified in their respective fields of specialization — primarily fit-outs, electro-mechanical, power, facilities, and industrial sales.

Since its inception, HPH Technical Solutions has grown exponentially, evolving into one of the industry’s recognized solutions providers for a diverse range of clients across industries on a regional scale.

01 — Mission

To conduct business with honesty and integrity, guided by high ethical standards — continuously innovating and adapting to change, and pursuing excellence in every product and service to gain a lasting competitive advantage.

02 — Vision

To be a one-stop provider for sales and services in the Philippines — a business partner, not just a contractor.
