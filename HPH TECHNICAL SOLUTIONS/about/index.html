Company Profile
HPH Technical Solutions Incorporation was established in 2013 and is managed and supervised by Engineers and Facilities Professionals who are consistently trained in seminars and certified in their respective fields of specialization primarily in Fit-outs, Electro-Mechanical, Power, Facilities and Industrial Sales.

Since its inception in 2013, HPH Technical Solutions Incorporated has grown exponentially and has evolved to become one of the industry’s recognized solutions provider for a diverse number of clients in different industries on a regional scale.
