    How backup works

    1. 1 No flicker when the grid drops The battery takes over in 10 milliseconds or less, so lights, Wi-Fi and computers keep running without a restart.
    2. 2 Size it to what matters Each extension battery adds 4 kWh (B4000 Elite) or 5 kWh (B5000 Elite), up to four. Big loads like aircon use up the battery fastest.
    3. 3 Solar keeps you going With solar panels, the sun recharges the battery during the day, so long brownouts don't have to mean long nights without power.

    This simulation is simplified for illustration, not a quotation. It assumes the battery starts full, keeps 10% in reserve and loses 5% each way between the battery and your appliances, and that the appliances you pick run the whole time (in real life lights, fans and aircon switch on and off, so the battery often lasts longer). Backup limits are from the TSUN data sheets: 2,500 W for the MSU4000 Elite and 3,600 W for the MAU5000 Elite (Plug & Play version). Motors and compressors can briefly draw more when they start. Solar output follows a simple daily curve: 650 W panels making about 3,100 kWh a year per 4 panels on an average day, more when sunny and much less in a typhoon. After the battery runs out, the system restarts once the sun can run your appliances or the battery has recharged to 20%.
