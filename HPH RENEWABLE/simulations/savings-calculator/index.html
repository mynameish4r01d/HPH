    How we estimate this

    1. 1 Your usage from your bill We divide your bill by the price per kWh to estimate how much electricity you use each month.
    2. 2 Only the solar you use counts Solar saves money when your home uses it, during the day or later from a battery. We don't count selling extra power back to the grid.
    3. 3 Sized to fit, not oversized Each MX2250 kit (4 panels) makes about 3,100 kWh a year. We recommend enough kits to cover what you can actually use.

    These figures are estimates for illustration, not a quotation.
