About Company
Last updated: July 10, 2026
HPH Technical Solutions Inc.
Established in 2013
Since our establishment in 2013, HPH Technical Solutions Inc. has built a reputation on one core principle: delivering clever, innovative solutions to complex problems. For over a decade, we've combined technical expertise with a forward-thinking approach to help our clients navigate challenges and achieve lasting results.
In 2025, we took that same spirit of innovation and applied it to one of the world's most pressing challenges — making clean, renewable energy accessible to everyone. This led to the founding of HPH Renewable, a dedicated division of HPH Technical Solutions Inc.
HPH Renewable
Established in 2025
HPH Renewable is a company driven by a clear advocacy: to accelerate the global shift toward renewable energy by making it more accessible and affordable for all. We believe that sustainable power shouldn't be a privilege reserved for a few — it should be within reach for homes, businesses, and communities everywhere.
Through quality service and forward-thinking, revolutionary products, we work to break down the barriers — technical, financial, and logistical — that have long stood in the way of widespread renewable energy adoption. Every solution we deliver is guided by a single mission: to make clean energy simple, affordable, and available to everyone.
We are committed to building a better tomorrow, today — and we invite you to be part of that journey.
Company Mission
Our mission is to make your home a net energy creator, delivering a future where clean power is effortless and your monthly electricity costs are reduced to their absolute minimum. Providing affordable clean and renewable energy to all.
Company Vision
Our vision is a cleaner renewable world that is not only affordable but accessible.
Advocacy
To solve the climate crisis and uplift communities, we must make clean energy not just available, but genuinely affordable to all people.
Driving Force
We believe in creating solutions so brilliant, they illuminate the path for others letting our good shine so brightly that the world sees a clearer, more sustainable way forward through God’s goodness.
A division of HPH Technical Solutions Inc.
