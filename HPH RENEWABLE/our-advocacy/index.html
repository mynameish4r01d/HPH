Take the Greed from the Grid.

We're fighting for Filipino energy independence. Every home should be able to make its own clean power at a price it can afford, instead of depending on rising rates and an unreliable grid.

Schedule a Visit Why we do it
expand_more

Why it matters

Electricity shouldn't be something Filipino families worry about every month.

  • receipt_long Bills keep going up Electricity is one of the biggest monthly costs for many households and small businesses, and families have no say in what the rate will be next month.
  • power_off Brownouts disrupt lives When the grid goes down, work stops, food spoils and homes go dark. Families shouldn't have to wait for power to come back.
  • diversity_3 Clean power is out of reach for many Solar has a reputation as something only the well-off can afford. Sustainable power shouldn't be a privilege for a few.

What we stand for

  1. 1 Affordable for every home We make renewable energy as affordable and accessible as we can, for every home, business and community we serve.
  2. 2 Energy independence We help families take back control of their power, so their home becomes a net energy creator instead of a customer at the mercy of the grid.
  3. 3 A cleaner Philippines We picture a country where every rooftop makes its own power and runs on sunlight. Each installation is a step toward it.
  4. 4 Honest advice We'd rather tell you what you actually need than sell you more. Clean energy only spreads if people can trust the people installing it.

How we act on it

  • sell
    Clear prices up front

    Our product pages show starting prices, and every estimate is labelled as an estimate. Your final quote comes after we've seen your home.

  • tune
    Sized to what you use

    We start from your last electricity bills and recommend the system that pays off for your home, not the biggest one we can sell.

  • verified
    Built to last

    We use micro inverters, so one fault doesn't stop your whole system, and LiFePO4 batteries designed for 10,000 cycles.

  • smartphone
    You can see it working

    The TSUN Smart app shows what every panel is producing, so you always know your system is doing its job.

  • school
    Free tools to learn from

    Our simulations let anyone explore how solar works, before talking to anyone. Start with How Solar Panels Work.
