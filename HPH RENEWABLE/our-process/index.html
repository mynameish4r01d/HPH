From First Visit to First Sunlight.

How we design and quote your system, and what happens from your first site visit to installation day and after.

Schedule a Visit See the steps
expand_more

The process, step by step

Every step before you accept your quote is free and comes with no obligation.

Before you commit Free, no obligation

  1. 1

    Book a site visit

    Fill in the Schedule a Visit form and choose a preferred date. If you can, upload your last 3 electricity bills. We use them to see how much power your home uses, so your quote is accurate.

  2. 2

    Site assessment

    We visit your home or property and check your roof, which way it faces, any shading, and how much electricity you use. If anything needs preparing before installation, such as safe roof access, we'll tell you here. No obligation and no hard sell.

  3. 3

    We design your system in OpenSolar

    Back at the office, we use what we measured to build your roof in OpenSolar, a professional solar design tool. We place the panels, account for shading, and size the system to what your home actually uses. See how it works.

  4. 4
    Within a week

    You get an itemized quote

    Within a week of your visit, you get a complete quote that lists the equipment and installation line by line, with the down payment terms and payment schedule. We walk you through it before you commit to anything.

After you say yes

  1. 5

    Down payment and scheduling

    Once you accept the quote and make the down payment, we book your installation date. You can pay by credit or debit card through PayMongo, or by bank transfer.

  2. 6
    1–2 days

    Installation

    A standard home installation usually takes 1 to 2 days, depending on system size and roof. Commercial jobs can take longer. The work is done by HPH TSI engineers and accredited installers, and qualified professionals supervise every job.

  3. 7

    Testing, app setup and handover

    We test the whole system and set up the TSUN Smart app on your phone. Then we show you how to use it, so you can see what every panel is producing.

  4. 8

    After-sales support

    We're your local support. If something comes up, call us. For hardware issues under warranty, we deal with TSUN and Projoy for you. We'll also tell you how to keep your system in good shape.

How we design your system

Your quote comes from a design of your actual roof in OpenSolar, not a rough guess.

  • roofing
    Your roof, modelled

    We build your roof's size, angle and direction from what we measured on site.

  • wb_shade
    Shading accounted for

    Trees, walls and nearby buildings are taken into account, so panels go where they'll produce the most.

  • tune
    Sized to what you use

    We match the system to your bills so you use the power you make, instead of relying on net metering.

What's in your quote

One complete, itemized quote, so there are no surprises later.

  • solar_power Equipment The inverters, panels, batteries and accessories your system needs, with each item listed. Prices are confirmed on the day the quote is issued, because equipment costs can change.
  • construction Installation Installation is quoted separately, because every roof is different. It depends on the number of panels, your roof type, and any electrical work needed.
  • payments Payment terms The down payment and payment schedule. Paying by credit card may let you use your bank's installment programs.

The prices on our product pages are starting prices. Want a rough idea first? Read the FAQ.
